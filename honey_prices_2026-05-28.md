## Key Market Observations (May 2026)

1. **Ukrainian honey dominates the low end** — 14.00-15.50 PLN/kg for sunflower and multi-flower in bulk barrels (200L), with minimum orders of 5-10 tons.
2. **Polish honey commands a premium** — 16-23 PLN/kg for domestic multi-flower, buckwheat, and linden, typically in 14-33kg buckets rather than barrels.
3. **Honeydew (spadziowy) is the most expensive common type** — 26 PLN/kg, reflecting lower yields.
4. **Heather (wrzosowy) remains premium** — 50 PLN/kg (Pasieka24 official table).
5. **Wholesale-to-retail markup is roughly 2.5-3x** — Wholesale multi-flower at 14-20 PLN/kg → retail 42-58 PLN/kg (Pasieka24 Dec 2025 table).
6. **Prices are relatively stable** — Comparing Pasieka24 Oct 2025 vs Dec 2025 tables: wholesale prices shifted only 1-2 PLN/kg across most types.
