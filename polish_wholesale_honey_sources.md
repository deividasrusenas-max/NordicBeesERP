## 1. PASIEKA24.PL — Official Industry Price Tables (BEST SOURCE)

**URL:** https://pasieka24.pl  
**What it is:** Digital version of "Pasieka" magazine — Poland's leading beekeeping trade journal.  
**Why use it:** Publishes the ONLY official bi-weekly wholesale (skup) and retail price tables covering ALL major honey types. Data is collected from market surveys across Poland.  
**Data format:** Structured price table with retail + wholesale prices per honey type.  
**Update frequency:** Every ~2 weeks (published in each magazine issue). Latest: Feb 8, 2025.

## 2. PSZCZOŁY.PL — Beekeeping Exchange Board (REAL-TIME)

**URL:** https://www.pszczoly.pl/gielda/  
**What it is:** Poland's oldest and largest beekeeping classifieds/exchange board.  
**Why use it:** Active daily listings where beekeepers and traders post actual selling/buying prices. Best source for real-time market pulse.  
**Data format:** Text-based classified ads with prices in PLN/kg.  
**Update frequency:** Daily (new listings added constantly).  
**Login:** Browse = no, Post = yes.  
**Typical prices found:**
- Akacjowy: 15-30 zł/kg
- Rzepakowy: 15-23 zł/kg
- Wielokwiatowy: 14-23 zł/kg
- Spadziowy: 20-25 zł/kg
- Lipowy: 15-25 zł/kg
- Gryczany: 15-22 zł/kg

## 14. USDA NATIONAL HONEY REPORT (International Reference)

**URL:** https://www.ams.usda.gov/mnreports/fvmhoney.pdf  
**What it is:** US Department of Agriculture monthly global honey trade report.  
**Why use it:** Includes US honey export data TO Poland (in USD). Provides international context for Polish market.  
**Data format:** PDF with trade tables (export/import volumes, values).  
**Update frequency:** Monthly (last issued: April 30, 2026 for March 2026 data).  
**Login:** No.  
**Poland data example (March 2026):**
- Export to Poland: 17 units, value $650
- Covers: comb honey, natural honey (organic, amber/darker categories)
- **Note:** Prices in USD, not PLN. Only US export data — does not cover EU-internal Polish market prices.

1. **For official historical price data:** PASIEKA24.PL (bi-weekly tables)
2. **For real-time market prices:** PSZCZOŁY.PL + IGRIT.PL (active exchanges)

4. **For market context/news:** TYGODNIK ROLNICZY + AGROPOLSKA
5. **For international context:** USDA National Honey Report (USD data)
